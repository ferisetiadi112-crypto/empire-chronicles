# World Model

Empire Chronicles is modern-day only.

## Two connected maps

### Home Country Map

A detailed operational map containing:

- settlements
- government offices
- housing
- industry
- agriculture
- infrastructure
- services
- population activity

### World Map

A strategic geopolitical map containing:

- countries
- borders
- diplomacy
- trade
- foreign investment
- international events
- conflicts

## Population

Military personnel, workers and unemployed people are part of the country's total population. They are not additional population pools.
